MLA vs MP vs corporator: who fixes your road?

Roads inside a city, potholes, street lights and drains are the municipal corporation's job, so your first call is your corporator. State highways sit with the state PWD, where the MLA has a say. National highways are the Centre's, through NHAI. An MP gets ₹5 crore a year under MPLADS but can only recommend works; the district administration carries them out.

  • Constitution of IndiaArticle 243W: powers of municipalities, tied to the matters in the Twelfth Schedule↗
  • Anantam IASTwelfth Schedule: the 18 municipal subjects; entry 4 is "Roads and bridges"↗
  • Drishti IASMPLADS: ₹5 crore per MP per year since 2011-12; MPs recommend, district authorities execute↗
  • Shankar IASNew MPLADS rules: the MP's role is limited to recommending a project↗
  • NHAINHAI: development, maintenance and management of national highways (NHAI Act, 1988)↗
  • WikipediaNational highways: owned by MoRTH, built and maintained by NHAI, NHIDCL and state PWDs↗
